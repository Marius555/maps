Embed harness

Renders the built embed against dev.json, a fixture snapshot — no Appwrite, no login, no publish. Source lives in embed/dev/ and is copied into the build output by Vite’s publicDir, so it never reaches a production deploy.

Run npm run build:embed, start the dev server, then open /embed/dev.html.

Checklist: pins coloured by category (uncategorised ones grey), the four locations with an icon drawn as large balls with a white glyph and the rest as small ones, every pin centred on its own coordinate, the pair beside the flagship clusters at low zoom (mixing both sizes into one bubble), clicking either one opens a popup clear of the pin, the category chips filter and refit, search narrows by name or address, a no-match search says so, and OpenStreetMap attribution shows exactly once. Set allowedDomains to something other than localhost and the map should refuse to render, warning to the console only.

Shapes: a blue circle around the flagship and an orange polygon over the old town, both drawn under every pin and cluster, both keeping their outline solid while the fill stays translucent. The circle must hold its ground distance across zoom levels — pinch in and out and it should cover the same streets, not the same number of pixels. Clicking a fill opens its card where you clicked; clicking a pin that sits inside a shape must still open the pin's card, not the shape's.

Results list: a panel of locations beside the map, with search and the filters docked at the top of it. Clicking a row opens that location's card and marks the row; clicking a pin scrolls its row into view and marks it. Narrow the browser window and nothing should change — the breakpoints are container queries, so squeeze the map's own box instead and it should stack with the map on top. “Nearest to me” re-sorts the whole list by distance rather than jumping to one pin.

Tags: two filter groups, “Sells” and “Open”, each under its own heading with no colour dot. Within a group the chips widen the results — ticking Bikes and Skis shows more shops than Bikes alone — and across groups they narrow: Bikes plus Sundays leaves only the flagship. That asymmetry is the whole rule; if ticking a second chip in one group ever removes a location, the matching is wrong.

Custom fields: the flagship's card carries a “Book a fitting” button beside Directions, plus “Dealer code” and “Workshop” as labelled rows — in the order the fixture defines them, not the order the values sit in. The button shows the owner's label, never the URL. Old town corner has only a dealer code, and the two locations with no values must show no field rows at all.

Search: typing filters the locations immediately, and after a beat offers Lithuanian places and postcodes underneath — run npm run build:gazetteer -- LT first, or the list stays empty and the console says why. Pick “Kaunas”, which has no location in it, and the map should fly there while the list re-sorts by distance from Kaunas rather than emptying. A chip under the controls says where the distances are measured from, and clears it. The whole control works from the keyboard: type, arrow down, Enter, Escape.

Appearance: the fixture sets labels: "some" and turns points of interest off and cycle paths on, so the basemap should carry city and district names but no street names, water names or shop markers, and dedicated cycleways should be drawn as green ribbons under the labels. All of it is applied to the style before the map is built — switch your OS to dark and reload, and the same three should still hold on top of the inverted basemap, because autoDark composes with them rather than replacing them.
