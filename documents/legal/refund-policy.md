  reminder we send is a courtesy, not an obligation.
- **(d) Upgrades and billing-period changes.** The amount charged for moving to a higher Plan, or

## 4. When a refund is given

A refund is given only in the following cases. The list is closed.

**4.1 Where the Terms promise one.** For the fees paid for any period after the date concerned, when:

- (a) a change materially reduces a feature of your paid Plan and you end your subscription before
  it takes effect (Terms §4.2);
- (b) we end the Agreement for convenience (Terms §12.3);
- (c) a force majeure event lasts more than 60 days and either of us ends the affected Plan
  (Terms §20.2); or
- (d) you end the Agreement before a change to the Terms that works against you takes effect
  (Terms §21.3).

**4.2 Billing errors.** We refund an amount charged by mistake: a duplicate charge for the same
period; a charge taken after a cancellation that we had confirmed took effect before it; or a charge
for a different Plan or billing period from the one you chose.

**4.3 Failure that is our fault.** If the paid features of your Plan are unavailable to you, through
our fault, for more than 7 consecutive days after you have told us about it in writing at
{{contact.supportEmail}}, we refund the part of the fee that covers the days from your notice until
they were restored — or, at our choice, give you a credit of the same value towards your next
payment. This does not apply to anything listed in §3.1(h) to (n), or to an interruption caused by
you, your website or your Account.

**4.4 Where the law requires one**, including under §1.3.

## 6. How a refund is made

**6.1** Refunds are paid through the Merchant of Record, to the payment method used for the original
payment, and in the currency it was made in. We do not pay refunds in cash, to another account or as
a transfer to a third party.

**6.2** A refund is never more than the amount actually paid for the period concerned, after any
discount, and excludes any amount the Merchant of Record has already refunded.

**6.3** A refund for a billing period ends the paid Plan for that period. Your Account then moves to
the Plan you have paid for, or to the Free Plan, and Terms §6.7 applies to content above that Plan's
limits.

**6.4** Once the Merchant of Record has issued a refund, how long it takes to reach you depends on
your bank or card issuer.

**9.3** §9.2 does not apply to a chargeback that succeeds because of a billing error under §4.2 that

**10.2** Cancelling is not a request for a refund, and does not lead to one (§3.1(a)).
